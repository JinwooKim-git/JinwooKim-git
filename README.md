## Projects

**CCP Dry Etching of SiO₂ — Electrode Configuration vs. O₂ Ratio**  
Industry-academia project with Tokyo Electron Korea · Mar. 2026 – Jun. 2026  

- Turned an equipment-survey assignment into an experiment: 6 conditions (PE / RIE × O₂ 10 / 20 / 30 %) on a single-frequency CCP etcher with CF₄/O₂
- Led experiment design, SEM measurement strategy, and data analysis; switched to 45° tilt SEM when cross-section cleaving failed
- Compared chemical (PE) and ion-assisted (RIE) etching regimes and their robustness to O₂ ratio

**Photolithography Recipe Recovery for MEMS Humidity Sensors**  
Soft MEMS Lab · Pt interdigitated electrodes on PET  

- After a lab relocation, the same lift-off recipe failed (Pt peeled off entirely)
- Used a short descum as a temporary fix, then traced the root cause to exposure dose loss from mask aligner lamp aging (lamp-time log)
- Re-established exposure time with 0.2 s split tests and removed the descum step — the recipe has held for over a year

**Sputter Qualification & Equipment Care**

- Co-wrote the operation manual for a newly installed DC/RF sputter system, now used as the lab's working manual
